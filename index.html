FAQ

Common questions.

Don't see your question? Get in touch and we'll be happy to help.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

Websites, cybersecurity documentation and reviews, IT support, presentations and business documents, Excel and data work, and custom projects that combine several of these.

How much does a project cost?

Pricing depends on scope, complexity and timeline. Share a short description through the inquiry form and you'll receive a written estimate before any work begins.

How long does a typical project take?

Timelines vary with scope. Smaller tasks can often be turned around quickly, while larger builds are planned in stages. Your estimate will include a realistic timeline.

What technology do you build websites with?

Most sites are hand-coded for speed, security and full ownership — no page-builder subscription required. Where a platform is a better fit for your needs, we'll recommend it and explain why.

Do I own the finished work?

Yes. Once the project is complete and paid for, the deliverables are yours, along with the files, access and notes you need to use them.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Ongoing maintenance and support can be arranged depending on the project. Terms are agreed separately so you only pay for what you need.
