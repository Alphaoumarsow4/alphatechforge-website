Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

This policy explains what personal information AlphaTechForge collects through this website, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

  • Inquiries: when you use the inquiry form, we receive your name, email address, the service you chose and your message.
  • Bookings: when you book a package, we receive your name, email address, the package and add-ons you chose, any notes you add, and the date you accepted our Terms of Service.
  • Payments: payments are handled by Stripe. Stripe collects your card or payment details directly; we only receive confirmation of the payment and limited details such as the last four digits of your card.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider may record standard server information, such as IP addresses and browser type, to keep the site secure and working.

How we use it

We use your information only to reply to you, prepare estimates, deliver and invoice your project, keep records the law requires, and protect the site from abuse. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Services we rely on

  • Cloudflare: hosts this website.
  • Resend: delivers inquiry form messages to our inbox.
  • Stripe: processes payments, under Stripe's Privacy Policy.

Cookies

This website doesn't use advertising or analytics cookies. Stripe's checkout page may use cookies needed to process payments securely and prevent fraud.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiries for as long as they are useful for replying and following up, and project and payment records for as long as tax and accounting rules require.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, unless we're required to keep it. Email alphatechforgestudio@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
